## Annotation coverage

- `sta.json` `spef_annotation`: 365 of 365 nets annotated at every corner, `unannotated_driver_count: 0`, `annotation_complete: true`. `20261018-92a63aa` reported 40 unannotated drivers (325 nets annotated); this record's klt extracts the other 40 nets, so that count does not carry over.
- SDF content (slow): 370 `CELL` entries, 1281 `IOPATH`, 661 `INTERCONNECT`.
- `environment.sdf.partial: true` in both reports. Dropped before annotation: 4 `TIMINGCHECK` sections (Icarus does not implement SDF timing checks, so setup/hold run against the cell library's placeholder values, not the characterised limits) and 370 zero-delay `INTERCONNECT` entries whose source is a bit-selected top-level input (Icarus cannot place them; every dropped entry has zero delay at every corner). Instance names with `[ ]` and `/` are renamed by klt (`renamed` in the report).

## What this does and does not claim

- Claims: on the post-route netlist with that corner's SDF delays, the cases above give the expected outputs at the 20 ns clock at the slow and fast corners.
- Does not claim: setup/hold (Icarus drops `TIMINGCHECK`; that stays with `klt sta`), the full vendored bench set (only two cases are ported), the typical corner, or an Fmax.
- Gap: `klt functional-verification` takes only cocotb benches, so the vendored Verilog benches could not be reused as they are. Filed as 2AMLogic/klayout-tools#4083.
